Harvest wood, brick and metal by swinging the pickaxe (slot 1) at trees, rocks, cars and any structure. Every build piece costs 10 mats and can be shot down. Only materials are collected by walking over them — point at a gun, ammo or a heal and press E / X to take it. Sprint toggles on and off and is double walking speed. Aiming in nudges the crosshair toward a nearby enemy. Stay ahead of the purple storm. Esc releases the mouse. A controller is picked up automatically — press any button and it takes over; the mouse is never grabbed while a pad is in use.
